Welcome to my corner of the internet. I'm Koosha — a software engineer and designer who lives at the intersection of code and craft.

## Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Arizona State University's Barrett Honors College. My journey started with graphic design in high school and evolved into a deep passion for building software that feels as good as it looks.

## What I Do

Currently, I lead product development at **Phenotype**, where I'm building a multi-agent software organization from the ground up. Before that, I designed and produced custom mechanical keyboard keycap sets — most notably GMK Arch, inspired by Arch Linux's minimalist aesthetic.

## The Mechanical Keyboard Journey

Mechanical keyboards are where my love for design and engineering collide. Every keycap set I design starts as a sketch, goes through dozens of iterations, and ends up as a physical product in people's hands. There's something deeply satisfying about holding something you designed — feeling the weight, the texture, the way light catches the legends.

That same philosophy drives my software work: ship things people can feel, not just use.

## What You'll Find Here

This blog is where I write about:

- Mechanical keyboard design and production
- Software engineering insights from a one-person org
- The intersection of design and code

Thanks for being here. Let's build something great.
